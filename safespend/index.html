SafeSpend — Privacy Policy

Daily spend limit, risk fund and daily savings for Android

The short version
  • SafeSpend does not collect, transmit or sell your personal or financial data.
  • There is no account and no login.
  • There are no ads, no analytics and no trackers.
  • Your records are stored only in the app's private storage on your device.
  • The only network request the app makes is a daily currency-exchange-rate lookup.

1. Who we are

SafeSpend is developed and published by Codex Zubair (legal entity: Shadow Ltd), operating through ryzadev.com. For any privacy question, contact support@ryzadev.com.

2. What SafeSpend stores on your device

All of your financial information — wallets and their balances, expenses, your survival plan, risk fund, savings, upcoming funds and app settings — is written to a single data file inside the app's private storage area on your device. Other applications cannot read it.

This data is used solely to show you your own information and to calculate your daily limit, savings rollover and fee previews. It is never uploaded to us, because we operate no server that receives it.

3. Network access

SafeSpend requests the Android INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE permissions for exactly one purpose: fetching current currency-exchange rates so that wallets held in different currencies can be combined into your main currency.

  • The request goes to a public exchange-rate service and contains only the currency code being requested (for example USD).
  • It contains no personal information, no identifier for you or your device, and no information about your balances, wallets or expenses.
  • At most one request is made per day, and only when you hold a wallet in a currency different from your main currency.
  • Rates are cached on your device so the app keeps working offline. If the request fails, the last saved rates are used and the app shows how old they are.
  • You may override any rate manually, and the app will then stop treating the fetched figure as authoritative for that currency.

As with any request to a third-party service, that service will necessarily see your IP address in order to respond. We do not control and are not responsible for that service's own logging practices.

4. What SafeSpend does not do

  • No advertising and no advertising SDKs.
  • No analytics, crash-reporting or attribution SDKs.
  • No user accounts, no sign-in, no passwords.
  • No access to your contacts, photos, files, camera, microphone, location, SMS or call logs.
  • No linking to your bank, card or mobile-money accounts. Balances are entered manually by you and are not verified against any institution.
  • No profiling, and no automated decisions about you.

5. Backup and your control over the data

SafeSpend works with Android's built-in backup so your records can be restored if you reinstall the app or move to a new device signed in to the same Google account. Android encrypts that backup and it is managed by Google under Google's privacy policy; we never receive a copy. You can turn Android backup off in your device settings at any time.

You can also export your data to a JSON file at any time from Settings → Export backup, and restore it with Import backup. The exported file is written wherever you choose to share it, and is then outside SafeSpend's control.

To delete your data, archive or delete individual records in the app, or clear the app's storage in Android settings, or simply uninstall SafeSpend. Because no copy exists on any server, uninstalling removes the data permanently. There is no account for us to delete.

6. Financial information and advice

SafeSpend is a personal record-keeping and budgeting tool, not a bank, lender, payment service, investment service or financial adviser. It does not hold, move or transfer money and does not connect to any financial institution. The daily limit, savings and fee figures are arithmetic performed on the numbers you enter. They are informational and are not financial advice.

Currency conversion, exchange-rate and fee results are estimates based on rates you or a public service provide. Actual amounts charged by your bank, mobile-money provider or freelance platform may differ.

7. Children

SafeSpend is a general-audience money-recording tool. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

8. International users

The app functions the same everywhere. Because your data stays on your device, there is no international transfer of your records by us. The currency-rate service you indirectly contact is operated by a third party, and your use of that feature is subject to its own terms.

9. Data safety summary (Google Play)

  • Does the app collect or share user data? No.
  • Is data encrypted in transit? The only network request uses HTTPS and carries no user data.
  • Can users request deletion? There is nothing held by us to delete; users delete their own data in-app or by uninstalling.
  • Permissions declared: INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE (exchange rates only).

10. Changes to this policy

If a future version adds a feature that changes any of the above — for example optional cloud backup with sign-in — this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the change will be described in the app's release notes. The date below always reflects the current version.
