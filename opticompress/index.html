OptiCompress

Offline photo compressor, resizer & PDF maker · Kai Develops

Privacy Policy · Last updated: November 10, 2026

1. Who we are

OptiCompress (“the App”) is developed by Kai Develops (“we”, “us”, or “our”). This policy applies to the OptiCompress Android app. It does not replace the privacy terms for Google Play, or other services you may use.

In one sentence: OptiCompress compresses, resizes, converts and creates PDFs entirely on your device — your photos never leave your phone. The App is free and supported by ads served by Google AdMob, and it requires no account or sign-in.

2. Photos, documents, and Photo Picker

When you choose photos or documents, OptiCompress uses Android’s system Photo Picker to access only the items you select. The app does not need broad access to your entire photo gallery for this selection flow.

Compression, resizing, format conversion, comparison, and PDF creation are performed locally on your device. OptiCompress does not upload, transmit, or store your selected photo or document content on Kai Develops servers or a photo cloud service. The core photo tools can work without an internet connection.

Photo metadata is also processed locally. You can preserve supported camera details or remove GPS coordinates before sharing, using the controls in the app.

3. Files and local history

When you save or export a result, the file is written to the location you choose on your device. The app may keep local processing history and storage-saving totals so you can find prior results. These records are not sent to Kai Develops servers.

  • Delete exported photos or PDFs from your device’s gallery or file manager.
  • Use the app’s History controls to remove individual records or clear multiple records.
  • Removing app data or uninstalling the app generally clears app-private local records. Files saved to shared device storage may remain and can be deleted separately. Android backup or device settings may affect local data behavior.

Because we do not keep server-side copies of your selected photos or local history, we cannot remotely retrieve or delete those device files for you.

4. Advertising and diagnostics (Google AdMob)

The app displays advertisements using Google AdMob and the Google Mobile Ads SDK. When ads are requested or shown, Google and its advertising or service partners may process device and ad-related information. Depending on SDK configuration, your region, consent choices, and device settings, this may include advertising or SDK identifiers, IP address, device and app details (such as device model, operating system, and app version), ad impressions or interactions, and diagnostic or crash information.

This information may be used to deliver or personalize ads where permitted, measure ad performance, prevent fraud or abuse, and operate the advertising service. The selected photos and documents are not sent to AdMob as part of OptiCompress’s local image-processing feature. Google’s processing is governed by Google’s own terms and privacy disclosures, including its Privacy Policy and information about how Google uses data from apps that use its services.

The exact data processed can change with the SDK version, app configuration, regional requirements, and your settings. We review the app’s Google Play Data safety disclosures alongside this policy when those practices change.

5. Internet use and sharing

Internet access may be used by the ad SDK to request or measure ads and by your device to access Google Play or other services you choose. Photo processing itself does not require an internet connection. Kai Develops does not sell your photos or share their contents with third parties.

6. Security

Keeping photo processing on your device means OptiCompress does not create a server-side copy of the photos you select. Advertising and diagnostic information handled by Google is subject to Google’s security practices and terms. Keep your device and app updated and use its built-in security controls.

7. Children

OptiCompress is a general-purpose photo utility and is not designed to collect personal information from children through Kai Develops-operated servers. Advertising treatment may depend on the app’s audience settings, applicable requirements, and Google’s controls.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when app features, advertising SDKs, legal requirements, or data practices change. The latest version will be published on this page with an updated date. Please review it from time to time.
