CoreSizer

CCTV & Server Sizing Calculators · AFLY Cloud LLC

Privacy Policy · Last updated: October 21, 2026

1. Overview

CoreSizer (“the App”) is a free, ad-free, fully offline calculator app for CCTV storage planning and server sizing. The App is published by AFLY Cloud LLC (“we”, “us”). This Privacy Policy explains what information the App does and does not collect when you use it. By using CoreSizer you agree to this policy.

In one sentence: CoreSizer collects no personal data, contains no advertising or analytics, requests no Android permissions, and never connects to the internet.

2. Information We Collect

The App does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information or usage data.

  • No account, sign-in, or registration is required.
  • No names, email addresses, phone numbers, or contacts are collected.
  • No location data is collected.
  • No device identifiers, advertising IDs, or IP addresses are collected.
  • No analytics, crash-reporting, or tracking tools are used.
  • We do not know what you calculate or what values you enter.

3. Internet Access

CoreSizer does not request or use the Android INTERNET permission. The App is technically incapable of connecting to the internet. All storage, bandwidth and sizing calculations are performed entirely on your device.

4. Permissions

CoreSizer requests no Android permissions at all. It does not access the camera, microphone, location, storage, contacts, phone, SMS, or network. The only optional interaction with an external app is opening the Privacy Policy link on the About screen or the Contact option, which opens your browser or email app only when you explicitly tap them.

5. Data Stored on Your Device

CoreSizer stores no data at all. The values you enter and the results you see exist only while the App is open and are discarded when it closes. Nothing is written to disk, and there is no history or profile.

6. Third Parties

The App contains no third-party advertising, no third-party analytics, and no third-party SDKs that collect data. The App uses the open-source url_launcher plugin solely to open links you explicitly tap. No data is shared with anyone.

7. Children's Privacy

CoreSizer is a professional utility suitable for all ages. Because it collects no data at all, it does not knowingly collect information from children or anyone else.

8. Data Security

Since no data leaves your device and none is transmitted, there is no data for us to secure or for a third party to breach.

9. Accuracy of Calculations

CoreSizer provides estimates based on standard industry bitrate and workload assumptions. Results are planning guidance, not a guarantee of performance. Always confirm final specifications against the equipment and software you actually deploy.

10. Open Source

CoreSizer's complete source code is public at github.com/codex-zubair/coresizer. Anyone may inspect the code to verify these privacy claims.

11. Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be included in a future app update and published on this page.
