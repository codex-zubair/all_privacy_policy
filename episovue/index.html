Privacy Policy · Last updated: October 17, 2026

1. Overview

Episovue (“the App”) is a free, ad-free, fully offline drama cliché bingo game for Android, published by codex-zubair (“we”, “us”). This Privacy Policy explains what information the App does and does not collect when you use it. By using Episovue you agree to this policy.

In one sentence: Episovue collects no personal data, contains no advertising or analytics, requests no permissions, and never connects to the internet.

2. Information We Collect

The App does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information or usage data.

  • No account, sign-in, or registration is required.
  • No names, email addresses, phone numbers, or contacts are collected.
  • No location data is collected.
  • No device identifiers, advertising IDs, or IP addresses are collected.
  • No analytics, crash-reporting, or tracking tools are used.
  • We do not know which squares you mark or how you play.

3. Internet Access

Episovue does not request or use the Android INTERNET permission. The App is technically incapable of connecting to the internet. The entire cliché list is bundled inside the App; nothing is downloaded, streamed, uploaded or synced.

4. Permissions

Episovue requests no Android permissions at all. It does not access the camera, microphone, location, storage, contacts, phone, SMS, or network. The only optional interaction with an external app is opening the GitHub or Privacy Policy links on the About screen, which opens your web browser only when you explicitly tap them.

5. Data Stored on Your Device

Episovue keeps a small amount of data locally so your progress between sessions can be shown:

  • The number of bingo cards you have started.
  • The number of games you have won.

All of this is stored in the App's private storage on your device using the operating system's preferences store. It is never uploaded, synced, or shared. Resetting it in the App's About screen, or uninstalling the App, permanently deletes it. The squares you mark on a card exist only while the App is open.

6. Third Parties

The App contains no third-party advertising, no third-party analytics, and no third-party SDKs that collect data. The App uses the open-source url_launcher plugin solely to open links you explicitly tap. No data is shared with anyone.

7. Children's Privacy

Episovue is a general-audience game suitable for all ages. Because it collects no data at all, it does not knowingly collect information from children or anyone else.

8. Data Security

Since no data leaves your device and none is transmitted, there is no data for us to secure or for a third party to breach.

9. Content

Episovue's clichés are short, original, generic descriptions of common drama tropes. The App contains no video, audio or other copyrighted media, and no verbatim quotations, dialogue or lyrics.

10. Open Source

Episovue's complete source code is public at github.com/codex-zubair/episovue. Anyone may inspect the code to verify these privacy claims.

11. Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be included in a future app update and published on this page.
